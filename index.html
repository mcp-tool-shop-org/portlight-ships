portlight-ships · spin test

Every state spins in lockstep on one clock. Watch for: scale jump (a state larger/smaller than its neighbours against the dashed box) · anchor drift (hull wandering off the crosshair as it turns) · flicker (a heading darker than the rest) · silhouette pop (shape discontinuity between adjacent headings).

This is a proxy for an in-engine spin test, not a substitute — it exercises the sprites the way a renderer would, but it is not the renderer.
